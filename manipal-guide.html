MIT Manipal vs MIT Bengaluru:
the established town campus or the new metro one?

A deep-research comparison of the two Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) engineering campuses — admission route, fees, branches, NIRF rank, placement reality, MET cutoffs, and an honest established-vs-new read. Backed by 2025–26 data and adversarially verified (refuted claims thrown out), not college-marketing fluff. General research — not tied to any specific applicant.

MIT Manipal · est. 1957 MIT Bengaluru · est. 2021 Route: MET + JEE Main Data: 2024–2026 cycles
30-second read. Both campuses belong to the same university (MAHE, a deemed university) and admit B.Tech the same way — the Manipal Entrance Test (MET), not KCET/COMEDK. MIT Manipal is the 1957-founded flagship: NIRF Engineering #59, NAAC A++, ~8,200 B.Tech students, a wide branch list, and a long proven placement track record. MIT Bengaluru is a small, computing-focused 2021 campus whose first batch only graduated in 2025 — far easier to get into (CSE closes ~3–9× higher rank), metro location, but a placement record that is still being written. Pick Manipal for brand, ranking and proven outcomes; consider Bengaluru for a Bangalore address, a computing-only focus, and an easier entry — accepting newer-campus risk.
A note on the data. Placement numbers are the weakest, most-inflated area in this whole space. During verification 9 of 25 headline claims were refuted — including widely-quoted figures like "86% placed / ₹12.31 LPA avg," "95%+ placement," and "₹69.25 LPA highest." Everything below is what survived a 3-vote adversarial check; figures that didn't are explicitly flagged. MAHE does not officially publish branch-wise packages, so CS-vs-core splits are aggregator estimates, not institute numbers.

02How you get in — the MET route

Identical mechanism for both campuses. No KCET, no COMEDK.

  • Manipal Entrance Test (MET) is the primary route for both campuses — MAHE is a deemed university that runs its own exam.
  • Merit for the main MET seats is weighted 50% MET score + 50% Class 12 marks.
  • A separate 20% of General seats per branch are filled via the JEE Main Common Rank List — so a JEE Main rank is a second door in.
  • Eligibility: Class 12 Science with a mandatory minimum (~50% PCM aggregate).
  • Neither campus uses KCET or COMEDK — a common point of confusion for Karnataka applicants.
Verified (3-0): the MET route, the 50:50 MET-board weighting, the 20% JEE Main General-seat channel, and the exclusion of KCET/COMEDK all confirmed across the primary MAHE Bengaluru program page plus Careers360 and aggregators.
Refuted — don't trust: a circulating claim that eligibility is "45–50% aggregate with a ₹2,000 application fee" was refuted 0-3. Verify the exact current cut-off percentage and fee on the official MAHE admissions portal before relying on a number.

Why two different placement rates float around. NIRF reports the enrolment-year cohort → ~65% for 2023-24 graduates. The institute's own 2025 drive materials cite ~86% using a different denominator/year. Both can be "true" for different cohorts — which is exactly why a single headline percentage is meaningless without the cohort definition.
Refuted — do NOT quote these: "86% placed / ₹12.31 LPA avg / ₹10.05 LPA median / ₹69.25 LPA highest" (0-3); "95%+ placement, ₹56 LPA highest" (0-3); "~80% placement, ₹69.25 LPA highest, ₹10.5 LPA median/avg" (0-3). All three were knocked out in verification.

08Established vs New — what you're really trading

The whole decision compresses to this.

Pick MIT Manipal if you want…

The proven flagship
  • A recognised brand & a real NIRF rank (#59) on the degree
  • A decades-deep alumni network and recruiter pipeline
  • The widest branch choice (incl. core engineering)
  • A large, pan-India peer group (~77% from outside Karnataka)
  • Proven, multi-year placement data to judge by

Consider MIT Bengaluru if you want…

The new metro bet
  • A Bangalore address — India's tech capital, internships next door
  • A computing-only focus (4 of 7 branches CS-family)
  • Materially easier entry (CSE ~9.4k vs ~1–3.5k rank)
  • A smaller, newer cohort and modern setup
  • …and you accept an unproven placement record and no own ranking yet
The trap to avoid: choosing MIT Bengaluru because "it's Manipal" and assuming the brand, ranking and placements transfer automatically. They don't — those attach to the main campus or to MAHE the university. Bengaluru's case stands on location + easier entry + computing focus, not on inherited reputation. If those three genuinely matter to you, it's a reasonable bet; if you're buying the name, the name lives in Manipal.

09The parent's lens

Beyond the brochures — what to actually check before you commit.

  • Same university, same accreditation umbrella. Both are MAHE (deemed, NAAC A++). The degree-issuing body is identical — that part is safe either way.
  • Get Bengaluru's fees in writing. No reliable public 4-year fee total survived verification — call the MAHE Bengaluru admissions office and get a branch-wise number on letterhead before budgeting.
  • Don't budget on aggregator placement numbers. The most-quoted figures (86% / ₹12.31 LPA / ₹69 LPA highest) were refuted. Ask each campus for its own latest official placement report, with cohort definition.
  • For Bengaluru, ask how many batches have graduated and where they went. One batch (2025) is not a track record. Recruiter depth is the real risk on a new campus.
  • CS-vs-core matters more than campus. At Manipal, CS-family branches out-earn core (Mech) by a wide margin (~₹10–15 L vs ~₹6–8 L). Branch choice drives outcome more than the campus name.
  • Factor total cost-of-living. Bengaluru metro living costs run higher than Manipal-town; add hostel + mess (~₹1.5–2.5 L/yr) on top of tuition for the real number.
Bottom Line
For a general "which is the better engineering campus" call, MIT Manipal wins on every proven dimension — ranking, scale, branch choice, and a real placement record. MIT Bengaluru is a location-and-access play, not a like-for-like substitute.

Choose MIT Manipal if you can clear its tougher cutoff and want the established brand and outcomes. Choose MIT Bengaluru only if a Bangalore base, a computing-only focus, or the easier entry genuinely outweigh an unproven, single-batch track record — and never on the assumption that the main campus's reputation comes along for free. Whichever campus: at this university, your branch (CS-family vs core) shapes your outcome more than the campus name does.
